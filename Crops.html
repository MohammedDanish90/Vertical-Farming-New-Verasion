WHY IT MATTERS

Benefits and trade-offs

Vertical farming offers useful opportunities, but its success depends on how the system is designed and operated.

💧

Water efficiency

Recirculating systems can reduce water consumption, depending on the setup and comparison method.

📐

Space efficiency

Stacked growing levels can increase production per unit of floor area.

🗓️

Growing consistency

Controlled conditions can reduce dependence on outdoor weather and seasons.

⚡

Energy requirements

Lighting, pumps and cooling can consume substantial electricity and affect profitability.

💰

Operating costs

Equipment, labour, energy and maintenance must be considered before investing.

🚚

Local supply

Growing close to customers may shorten transport distances and improve freshness.
