Three ways to grow

Each technique has different requirements and works best for particular crops and environments.

01 / WATER BASED

Hydroponics

Plants grow without soil using water enriched with essential nutrients. The roots are supported by a suitable growing system.

  • Water and nutrient management
  • Suitable for leafy greens
  • Can recirculate water
02 / MIST BASED

Aeroponics

Plant roots are suspended and receive a fine mist containing water and nutrients.

  • Precise root-zone delivery
  • Requires reliable equipment
  • Needs careful monitoring
03 / CONTROLLED ENVIRONMENT

LED & Climate Control

Artificial lighting and environmental controls help provide consistent growing conditions indoors.

  • Adjustable light schedules
  • Temperature and humidity monitoring
  • Potential for year-round production
